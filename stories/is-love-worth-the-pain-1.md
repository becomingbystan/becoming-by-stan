I've spent the (almost) past three weeks crying and rotting in my bed ever since that boy dumped me. I'm not going to say I wasted 6 months of my life, of love, and of energy over that boy, all for it to be meaningless in the end. Even though I felt this way at first, and I still did yesterday, I want to think it was not meaningless. My friends tell me there's always a lesson to learn, and I know it, but somehow I can't get a hold on the lesson this was supposed to teach me. 

Even before he dumped me I was sad, and I was crying myself to sleep quite often, why? he was inconsistent. I knew he appreciated me, but he never used to say it, and I can't ask for him to say it, otherwise it wouldn't come from him (does that make sense?). I just couldn't wait to become his boyfriend, so that one day I could tell him I loved him and meaning it. I wanted to be able to picture a future with him without sounding delusional. yes, I was crying over him, but he was treating me right, but not enough. 

He told me I was needing more than he could ever give me. He also used to tell me how I was "too much", in everything, whether it was my emotions, my words or even actions. I was always too much.

Whenever I told him how awesome and pretty and hot and amazing of a person he was, he always said "I'm not all that Stan", and I know, I knew it already. I know all his downsides and red flags, all the things I hate about him. That was the beautiful part to me, I accepted all of it. Nobody's perfect, I also have downsides. but that's also the worst part, I wasn't idealizing him anymore, I wanted him for who he was, not an imaginary version of him I created inside my mind.

2 days ago I accepted his request, of being "friends", but I told him it'll never be a platonic friendship because there's always gonna be attraction and I'm always going to try to get him back, I told him "I'll win you back", but for that I need to show him I can be lighthearted again, give him space and time. I'll try to call him, if he ever accepts, and try to see him. 

He is the man I want, the one I've been praying for, and I'm not letting him go that easily. I'll try everything I can. So now the crying Stan stfu and the baddest b in town has to come back.
