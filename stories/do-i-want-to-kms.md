In my first post, I told you about how bad things were going for me. To be honest, life is not at its peak right now, and I know that brighter days are ahead. Deep down I know it. Yet, I still catch myself sometimes thinking that this life is too much of a burden to keep going and to keep fighting. I just want things to be easy. 

You want to know what the weirdest part is? I am scared of death. I literally think that I could never take my own life, because it genuinely scares me, but I still have this thought in the back of my head sometimes, and I've already had it before, it's not like it's the first time. Like a year ago I was also at my lowest, the loneliest I've ever been. I feel like my current situation is the worst one I've ever had to face, but I'm still in a sort of denial of everything that's happened to me. I'm still trying to get my bsf back, my boy back, and to romanticize my studies (I think I might quit really soon). So at the end of the day, to escape my reality, I picture the life I dream of, and I am married to that boy, we have kids and happily grow old together. But there's something denial, daydreaming and fighting can't get me back: my grandma. I crave to talk to her, get a hug from her, and smell her perfume from across the room again. Whenever we were together we would always hold hands as we were talking. I wanted to learn Portuguese so that I could talk even more to her, because as she got older, she was forgetting more and more French words. There are so many questions I never got to ask, so much of our family history I'll never get to know, I never even got to come out to her. She died thinking I was straight which hurts real bad. I don't even know if she would've accepted it, that's maybe why I never told her. I didn't want to lose her before I actually had to. But it happened, she died. I don't know how I'm supposed to keep going into a world I don't want to be apart of, when she's not here with me. I hope she's watching me from above and that she loves me and is proud of the man I'm growing up to be. I hope she's not too mad I want to quit law, because I know how much faith she had in me, and how much she had to sacrifice so she could give a better life to her children and grandchildren. She was my why and I will always love her. 

Eu sempre vou te amar <3
