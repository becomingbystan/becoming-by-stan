In my opinion, love is the most beautiful feeling a human being can ever experience in their lifetime. I dream of a relationship like they don't do anymore: a boy who sticks with me no matter what. A boy who loves through the ups and downs of life. A boy I can picture myself getting married to and have children with. 

I'm not asking for a Disney prince or a relationship straight out of a movie or a fairytale, I'm just asking for true love. I want a consistent boy whose love language is using words of affirmation, because it feels so good to be reminded how pretty you are, how happy you make that person and how often they think about you. I want a boy who will never have made me doubt myself, my worth and value. A boy who never made me feel like I was too much, because I know I am. When I feel something, I feel it with my whole heart. I also want a boy who's interested in truly getting to know me for real, who asks me about my day, because to be seen is to be valued and loved. I want a boy who's not here for sex, because I could make him wait until marriage just to be sure. I want to be the person this boy chooses at the end of the day, the person he loves every single day knowing he'll still love me the day after, and the one after. To me love is not only a feeling, it's also a choice, because when things get hard you can go with the flow and just leave because that's easier, but that's exactly when you need to fight and choose that person. An argument or a bad moment cannot be representative of months of relationship.

This all feels like a subliminal message for the boy I was in a situationship with, and ended everything after 6 months of everyday talking. I miss him, and I want him back. I've been fighting for a week, but I don't know how much longer I can do this, because if he doesn't want me anymore, I can't force him right? I think about him all the time and wish he would just give us a chance instead of throwing this all away. 

I should probably conclude thinking that my version of love is too deep to be realistic...

-xoxo, Stan
