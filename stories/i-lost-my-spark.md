hi!

I'm sorry to anyone who's about to read this post (if anybody ever reads it), it's not gonna be the most joyful one of my diary. 

I don't even know where to begin. 

As the title says, I lost my spark, but what is it exactly? It was me day dreaming, happily getting dressed, going out, having fun with my friends, watching my favorite movies, listening to music so loudly, drawing, baking... But how did I come to lose it? To be honest, I feel like September 2026 is probably like hell on earth. or maybe the whole of 2026? I don't know. I had happy moments this year, but what am I missing to be like this again? what happened? why am I like this? 

Past July my grandma passed away at 91. She was the love of my life and a role model for me. Only 2 people matter to me this much: her, and my mom (her daughter). When she passed away, on the 9th, my world fell apart. The day before her passing, prom took place, I was celebrating the end of high school and the results of the final exams. It all changed within 24 hours. What hurt the most is that I wasn't even able to attend her funerals. She lived in Portugal, and me in France. What do you mean I won't ever see my grandma ever again? I might not believe in God anymore, she did, so I find comfort in knowing she's peaceful now in Heaven, gathered with my grandpa (the wisest and calmest person this world has ever known). Since he passed away in 2023, she's been missing him so bad, she always said she wanted to be reunited with him. That's love. They were married for about over 60 or 70 years, isn't it crazy? 

In August I confessed to my straight bsf how much I liked him. I didn't tell him I had a crush on him because I don''t think that's what I felt in this moment, but I needed to be honest with him and tell him how I felt, or I would've ended up gotten hurt by keeping it a secret. I probably got this idea put into my head by the people surrounding me, telling me he was weirdly close to me knowing I am gay, and that they could definitely see something was going on between us. It's not my first rodeo, I already had a crush on several of my straight friends throughout the years, and I should've learned my lesson, but I didn't. This boy was the nicest I've ever known, he was caring, comforting, and never judging me. We were staying up until so late in the night while being on the phone for hours. He is the bestest friend I've ever had, and I mourn this loss every single day since I ruined everything. Because after I confessed, he obviously told me he never felt the same way romantically, but kept acting the same way towards me. It wasn't really healthy and my feelings weren't going away, so I told him, and he stopped answering to me. He stopped giving me any sign of life. He stopped being there for me, he stopped being one of the only constants in my life. 

Now's the part you'll think I'm a horrible person. There's another boy, the story is complex and deserves its own post (pinky promise). We were talking for 6 months (officially), being apart of each other's life. We shared a lot of our personal lives. I even told him about things I don't feel comfortable saying out loud, because I truly felt safe with him, and never judged. We also shared a bit of intimate moments, and I had put a lot of faith into that boy and what we could've become in the future. 4 days ago he dumped me. He said we were arguing too much by his standards, but what the hell? How could he throw away all that we had built in the past 6 months of our lives over something so meaningless? Considering I'll dedicate a post on our backstory, I won't make it any longer. Just know, I'm not giving up on this boy.

Finally, on August the 31st, I had my back to school AT UNIVERSITY?! Your boy is going to law school! What, like it's hard? No but seriously, my bachelor's degree is not only about French constitutional law, but also the common law, which was so interesting to me, considering my dream would be to live in London, or somewhere else in the UK (or in the USA but after Trump's presidency ends). I am a European baby created by immigration. My grandma gave birth to my mom in France (with the Portuguese nationality), and my dad's side is Polish and French but like poor. MY parents didn't make it to uni and stopped school very early in their lives, I know they deeply regret it because of how often they say it to me. Even though my life-long dream was to work in the fashion industry (preferably in marketing or communication), I wanted to secure a stable career, a stable life, and make my parents proud. So I conformed to society's expectations of me and of what I was supposed to be: the first one to go to uni, and the first lawyer (I don't even wanna be one like wtf?) in the family after all their sacrifices. Now, here I am, and I thought I would end up being passionate about it. I thought I could do it for my parents, but also for my future children, but I can't. I mean don't get me wrong, at first I came with a plan: I wanted to work in International Relations (IR), and get specialized in this field during my master's degree. But I miss my creativity so much. That's probably the reason why I even opened this blog in the first place: a way to do something I liked and reconnect to my through self, when all I want to do is escape the life I'm currently leading. I also miss my friends, my siblings, my parents, my home, my room, my cats, my hometown (trust me I never thought those words would ever come out of my mouth), to sum it all up: I miss my old life.

So to make it short and clear, my grandma passed away, my bsf stopped talking to me because I told him I liked him WHILE I was flirting with the boy I thought was the loml (meaning "love of my life" for the millennials), but who ended up dumping me after a 6 months long talking stage. And now I am in an unknown city, studying something I don't want to study to have a job I don't want. It can't get any worse from now on, right?
