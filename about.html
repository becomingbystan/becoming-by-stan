I'm Stan, and Becoming, by Stan is my personal digital diary — a place where I can put all the things that occupy my mind.

I love fashion, cinema, music, art, politics, world affairs, travelling, writing and discovering new things. I'm interested in far too many things to fit neatly into one box, so I decided to create a space where all of them could exist together.

Some posts will be about things I love. Some will be about things I'm trying to understand. Some will simply be thoughts I don't want to lose.

That's where Becoming comes from.

I don't think we're ever really finished becoming who we are. We're constantly changing, learning, discovering new people, new places, new ideas and new versions of ourselves.

This blog is a way of documenting some of that process.
