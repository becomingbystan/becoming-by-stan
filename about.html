I'm Stan, I'm an eighteen-year-old small town boy dreaming about a life bigger than him. I am french, so hopefully you'll forgive my grammar mistakes, but also portuguese (on my mom's side) with polish roots (on my dad's side, oh and fun fact Stan is from the polish name Stanisław). I am gay so don't get a jumpscare if u catch me talking about boys and saying how hot and evil they are!!! Becoming, by Stan is my personal little digital diary. It feels like a place where I can put all the things that occupy my mind. Honestly, it kinda feels like a Hannah Montana lifestyle, and I'm obsessed!!

I love fashion, cinema, music, art, politics,travelling, discovering new things, and so much more. But if you want a quick sum up: I'm an art boyyyyy haha. I'm interested in far too many things to find my niche and fit into one box, so I decided to create a space where all of them could exist together (nah bcs seriously why not?).

Some posts will be about things I love, or hate. Some will be about things I'm trying to understand. Some will simply be thoughts I don't want to lose.

That's where Becoming comes from.

I don't think we're ever really finished becoming who we are. We're constantly changing, learning, discovering new people, new places, new ideas and new versions of ourselves, to become an ideal human being no one has ever reached, and never will. The goal is to become a bit better each day more than the previous one.

This blog is a way of documenting that process.
